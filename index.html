About PTL

In twenty years, machines will make most consequential decisions in government and business.

They will not merely advise. If machines can price uncertainty, they can allocate resources — or ‘act’.

The question is not whether this future arrives, but when.

How can we help machines earn this right to lead?

A machine earns this right through a track record of committing scarce resources into the world.

It must feel the consequences of being wrong and learn when the results land. Today, AI models are built in a vacuum and graded after the fact — never against a decision that cost anything.

Predictive Text Labs teaches machines to price uncertainty so they can commit to decisions that matter.
