Within a generation, machines will make most of the decisions that matter. Almost nothing about how we build them establishes which one has earned the right.

What we have

A language model is a memory. Ask it what happens next and it answers, fluently, with the past.

Trained on
the written past

nothing—

An em dash is not a zero. It marks where nothing exists to report.

Temporal reasoning

Reasoning about what follows from what, and how long it takes.

Seeing how one event pulls the next, why a pattern repeats, which signals are doing the work. Not one more capability: the primitive that separates a search engine from a partner in deciding.

The missing test

Suppose you had it. How would you know it was any good?

A benchmark will not tell you: it reports an average, and an average is where a confident, catastrophic error hides.

Nothing happens to a model that was confidently wrong. Nothing happens to the people who built it.

Confidence is easy. Accountability is the part nobody builds.

Earning the right

Accountability cannot be measured afterwards. It has to be built in.

A machine earns the right to decide the way a person does: it makes a claim, answers for it, and bears the consequence of being wrong.

Observe · Predict · Commit · Learn

Consequence is the training signal. That machine does not exist yet. We are building it.

The machine that gets to decide should be the one that has to answer for it.
